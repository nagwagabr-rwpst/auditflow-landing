Internal audit operations in one Odoo 20 addon

Standard edition covers audit engagements, review results, corrective actions, and dashboard KPI cards. It is not a certified GRC platform and it does not include a separate risk-management product.

📋

Audit engagements

Move an engagement through Draft, Fieldwork, Reporting, Issued, Closed, and Cancelled. Record the entity under review, audit team, lead reviewer, dates, scope, and objectives. An issued engagement report can be printed.

📝

Review results

Record severity, execution status, owner, target date, impact, likelihood, and management response. Risk Rating on a review result is calculated from Impact and Likelihood.

✅

Corrective actions

Create corrective actions from review results and follow the stored states draft, assigned, in progress, completed, and cancelled. Delayed actions are flagged.

📊

Dashboard KPI cards

The executive dashboard shows KPI cards for engagements, review results, risk level, and corrective actions. The selected card opens a Bar Chart, Trend, or Distribution chart. Risk level counts review results in the current filters.

🎯

Dashboard filters

Filters are Reviewer, Entity under review, Execution status, and Risk level. They apply to KPI totals and the selected metric chart. Execution status filters review-result status. Risk level filters review-result severity.

🌍

English and Arabic

English is the source language, and the module ships with an Arabic translation catalog for audit operations, review results, plans, tasks, indicators, and the executive dashboard. Some report, portal, and search labels remain English. This is not complete bilingual coverage.
